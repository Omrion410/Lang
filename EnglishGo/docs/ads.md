# EnglishGo - Advertisement System

# Overview

This document defines the advertisement strategy used by the EnglishGo application.

The primary objective is to generate sustainable revenue while maintaining an enjoyable learning experience.

Advertisements should never interrupt the educational flow excessively.

---

# Objectives

The advertisement system should:

* Generate revenue.
* Preserve user experience.
* Reward user engagement.
* Avoid excessive interruptions.
* Encourage Premium subscriptions.

---

# Advertisement Types

Supported advertisement formats:

* Rewarded Video
* Interstitial Advertisement
* Banner Advertisement (Limited)
* Native Advertisement (Future)

---

# Rewarded Videos

Users may voluntarily watch advertisements to receive rewards such as:

* Extra XP
* Coins
* Bonus Hearts
* Daily Reward Multiplier
* Lesson Retry
* Premium Trial Rewards (Future)

Rewarded ads are optional.

---

# Interstitial Advertisements

Interstitial ads may appear:

* After completing several lessons.
* After finishing a quiz.
* After claiming certain rewards.

They should never appear:

* During a lesson.
* During an exercise.
* During speaking or listening practice.

---

# Banner Advertisements

Banner ads should be used carefully.

They may appear:

* Home Screen
* Store Screen
* Achievement Screen

Banner ads should never reduce usability.

---

# Advertisement Frequency

The application should limit advertisement frequency.

Examples:

* Maximum one interstitial after multiple completed lessons.
* Rewarded ads remain optional.
* No advertisement spam.

---

# Premium Experience

Premium users receive:

* No advertisements.
* Faster progression.
* Additional learning content.
* Exclusive rewards.

---

# Advertisement Providers

Potential providers include:

* Google AdMob
* Google Ad Manager (Future)
* Unity Ads (Future)
* AppLovin (Future)

---

# User Protection

Advertisements must:

* Be age appropriate.
* Respect privacy.
* Follow platform policies.
* Avoid misleading content.
* Never force accidental clicks.

---

# Analytics

Track:

* Ad Impressions
* Rewarded Ad Views
* Click Rate
* Revenue
* Fill Rate
* eCPM
* User Retention Impact

---

# Future Improvements

Future advertisement features:

* Personalized Ads
* Regional Optimization
* AI-based Ad Timing
* Premium Upgrade Offers
* Seasonal Campaigns

---

# Design Principles

The advertisement system should always remain:

* Respectful
* Optional where possible
* Non-intrusive
* Profitable
* User Friendly
* Scalable
