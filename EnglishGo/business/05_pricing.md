# EnglishGo - Pricing System

# Overview

The Pricing System defines the pricing strategy for Premium subscriptions while ensuring affordability, flexibility, and sustainable business growth.

---

# Objectives

* Fair Pricing
* Global Accessibility
* Flexible Subscription Options
* High User Value
* Sustainable Revenue

---

# Pricing Strategy

EnglishGo should offer competitive pricing based on:

* Country
* Purchasing Power
* Platform Policies
* Promotional Campaigns

---

# Subscription Plans

Available plans:

* Monthly
* Quarterly
* Yearly

The yearly plan should provide the highest value.

---

# Regional Pricing

Prices may vary depending on:

* Country
* Currency
* Local Economy

Regional pricing should remain fair for every market.

---

# Student Discounts

Eligible students may receive:

* Discounted Premium Plans
* Seasonal Educational Offers

---

# Family Plans

Future family plans may allow:

* Multiple Users
* Shared Subscription
* Individual Learning Progress

---

# Promotional Pricing

Support:

* Seasonal Discounts
* Limited-Time Offers
* First-Time Subscriber Discounts
* Anniversary Promotions

---

# Free Trial

Every new user receives:

* 14 Days Premium Free

No Premium features should be permanently locked before the trial ends.

---

# Upgrade Value

The Premium subscription should clearly provide value beyond the free version through:

* AI Features
* Unlimited Learning
* Advanced Analytics
* Ad-Free Experience

---

# Future Plans

Planned pricing improvements:

* Lifetime Plan
* Enterprise Plan
* School Licenses
* Corporate Packages

---

# Design Principles

The Pricing System should always remain:

* Fair
* Flexible
* Transparent
* Competitive
* Scalable
* Future Ready
