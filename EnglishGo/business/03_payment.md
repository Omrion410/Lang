# EnglishGo - Payment System

# Overview

The Payment System manages all financial transactions securely and reliably.

It should support international payments while remaining simple and user-friendly.

---

# Objectives

* Secure Payments
* Fast Transactions
* Multiple Payment Methods
* Automatic Subscription Management
* Global Availability

---

# Supported Payment Methods

Initial Release:

* Google Play Billing
* Apple In-App Purchases

Future Support:

* Credit Cards
* Debit Cards
* PayPal
* Apple Pay
* Google Pay
* Regional Payment Methods

---

# Payment Security

The system should:

* Encrypt Transactions
* Never Store Card Information
* Follow Platform Security Standards
* Detect Fraudulent Payments

---

# Subscription Payments

The system supports:

* Monthly Subscription
* Quarterly Subscription
* Yearly Subscription

Payments renew automatically unless cancelled.

---

# Failed Payments

If payment fails:

* Notify the User
* Retry Automatically
* Allow Manual Retry
* Maintain Data Integrity

---

# Restore Purchases

Users can restore:

* Active Subscriptions
* Premium Features
* Previous Purchases

---

# Refunds

Refund requests follow the policies of:

* Google Play
* Apple App Store

---

# Payment Notifications

The system should notify users about:

* Successful Payment
* Failed Payment
* Subscription Renewal
* Subscription Expiration
* Refund Confirmation

---

# Future Features

Planned improvements:

* Family Payments
* Gift Subscriptions
* Promo Codes
* Discount Campaigns
* Regional Pricing

---

# Design Principles

The Payment System should always remain:

* Secure
* Reliable
* Fast
* Transparent
* Global
* Future Ready
