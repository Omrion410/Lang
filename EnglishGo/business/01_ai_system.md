# EnglishGo - AI System

# Overview

The AI System is the intelligent core of EnglishGo.

Its purpose is to provide every learner with a personalized English learning experience through speech analysis, pronunciation correction, conversation practice, adaptive learning, and continuous performance evaluation.

The AI should act as a personal English tutor available at any time.

---

# Objectives

The AI System should:

* Improve pronunciation.
* Build speaking confidence.
* Detect learning weaknesses.
* Adapt lessons to each learner.
* Encourage continuous improvement.
* Provide a realistic English conversation experience.

---

# AI Speaking Coach

The AI Speaking Coach evaluates spoken English.

Features include:

* Speech Recognition
* Pronunciation Analysis
* Word Accuracy Detection
* Sentence Accuracy
* Speaking Fluency
* Confidence Evaluation
* Progress Tracking

---

# Pronunciation Analysis

The learner reads a sentence aloud.

The AI compares the pronunciation with native pronunciation.

Results include:

* Overall Score
* Word Accuracy
* Fluency
* Speaking Speed
* Pronunciation Suggestions

Example:

Overall Pronunciation

92%

---

# Word-Level Feedback

Every spoken word is analyzed.

Example:

✅ Hello

✅ My

❌ Name

✅ Is

❌ English

Incorrect words should include pronunciation guidance and allow the learner to repeat them.

# AI Conversation

The learner can practice real conversations with AI.

Examples:

* Ordering Food
* Airport
* Hotel
* Shopping
* Job Interview
* Daily Conversation
* Emergency Situations

The AI responds naturally and evaluates the learner throughout the conversation.

# Adaptive Learning

* Weak Vocabulary
