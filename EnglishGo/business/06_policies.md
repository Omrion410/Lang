# EnglishGo - Business Policies

# Overview

This document defines the business policies governing subscriptions, payments, user rights, and commercial operations within EnglishGo.

The objective is to ensure fairness, transparency, legal compliance, and long-term business sustainability.

---

# Objectives

* Protect Users
* Protect EnglishGo
* Ensure Fair Business Practices
* Maintain Transparency
* Support Long-Term Growth

---

# User Rights

Every user has the right to:

* Access purchased features.
* Cancel subscriptions.
* Restore purchases.
* Request support.
* Report technical issues.

---

# Subscription Policy

Users may:

* Subscribe at any time.
* Upgrade plans.
* Downgrade plans.
* Cancel automatic renewal.
* Restore active subscriptions.

---

# Free Trial Policy

Every eligible new user receives:

* 14 Days Premium Free

The trial automatically ends after the defined period.

Premium access continues only with an active subscription.

---

# Payment Policy

Payments are processed through:

* Google Play
* Apple App Store

EnglishGo does not store users' payment card information.

---

# Refund Policy

Refund requests follow the policies of:

* Google Play
* Apple App Store

Where applicable, EnglishGo may provide additional customer support during the refund process.

---

# Advertisement Policy

Advertisements displayed inside the application should remain:

* Family Friendly
* Educational
* Respectful
* Safe

Advertisements containing inappropriate or harmful content should be filtered whenever possible.

---

# Privacy Policy

EnglishGo is committed to:

* Protecting User Data
* Encrypting Sensitive Information
* Collecting Only Necessary Data
* Respecting User Privacy

---

# Service Availability

The company aims to provide reliable service at all times.

Temporary maintenance or unexpected outages may occur when necessary to improve security, stability, or performance.

---

# Future Business Policies

Future additions may include:

* Corporate Accounts
* School Licenses
* Educational Partnerships
* Enterprise Agreements
* Regional Business Policies

---

# Design Principles

Business Policies should always remain:

* Fair
* Transparent
* Ethical
* Secure
* Reliable
* Future Ready

---
