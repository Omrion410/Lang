# EnglishGo - Subscription System

# Overview

The Subscription System defines how learners access free and premium features while ensuring a sustainable business model.

The system should provide value before requesting payment and encourage long-term subscriptions.

---

# Objectives

* Provide a generous free experience.
* Encourage Premium upgrades.
* Maintain fair pricing.
* Maximize learner retention.
* Support long-term business growth.

---

# Free Trial

Every new user receives:

* 14 Days Premium Free
* Full Premium Features
* No Advertisements
* Unlimited Lessons
* AI Speaking Coach
* AI Conversations
* Premium Analytics

---

# Trial Notifications

The system should notify users:

* 3 Days Remaining
* 1 Day Remaining
* Trial Expired

---

# Free Version

After the trial expires, users continue with:

* Basic Lessons
* Limited Exercises
* Basic Pronunciation Score
* Advertisements
* Limited Daily AI Usage

---

# Premium Version

Premium users receive:

* Unlimited Lessons
* Unlimited Exercises
* Full AI Speaking Coach
* AI Conversations
* Detailed Pronunciation Analysis
* Advanced Progress Reports
* No Advertisements
* Priority New Features

---

# Subscription Plans

Available plans:

* Monthly
* Quarterly
* Yearly

---

# Upgrade Promotion

The application should promote Premium naturally by:

* Showing locked premium features.
* Displaying learning progress.
* Highlighting Premium benefits.
* Offering upgrade reminders.

---

# Subscription Management

Users can:

* Subscribe
* Renew
* Cancel
* Restore Purchases
* Change Plans

---

# Design Principles

The Subscription System should always remain:

* Simple
* Transparent
* Fair
* Flexible
* Secure
* User-Friendly
